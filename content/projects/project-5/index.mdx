Investment Council runs investment due diligence as a multi-agent workflow. It pairs deep research with a "council" of agents that argue the opportunity from opposing sides before reaching a verdict you can act on.

## The Problem

Good investment research is slow, expensive, and biased toward optimism. A human analyst can spend weeks building a single report, and most reports still present only the bullish case. The questions that decide whether a position works are the ones that usually go unasked: what could go wrong, what risk is being overlooked, and what a contrarian would see that the author did not.

A single AI model does not fix this on its own. Ask one model to research a company and it tends to produce a confident, agreeable summary, the same blind spot as the over-eager analyst. The bias comes from having a single perspective with nothing pushing back on it. You end up making decisions on half the picture.

## The Solution

The platform breaks research into a structured pipeline where each stage has a clear job, and the agents are deliberately set against each other:

1. **Research.** Google Gemini Deep Research runs multi-step research across the company and its market, then synthesises it into a full report covering fundamentals, competitive position, and financials.
2. **Strategy.** A specialised agent re-reads that research through a chosen investment lens (Value, Special Situations, or Distressed), each with its own framework and metrics, and frames the actual opportunity.
3. **Challenge.** This is the heart of it. A Skeptic agent, modelled on a short seller, tears apart the bullish case: it flags optimistic assumptions, cherry-picked data, and hidden red flags. In parallel, a Risk Officer agent works through regulatory, operational, financial, market, macro, and ESG risk with a probability and impact view.
4. **Verdict.** A final agent weighs the research, the strategy view, the skeptic's attack, and the risk assessment, then returns an INVEST, PASS, or WATCH call with a conviction score. INVEST needs genuine conviction, WATCH means interesting but not yet, PASS means the risk-reward does not hold up.

After the verdict, an interrogation step lets you question any part of the analysis with the full context still loaded, so you can press on a specific risk or scenario before committing capital.

## What It Does

In practice it supports two ways of working. You can start from a thesis, for example "undervalued small-cap tech trading below book value," and let the system discover and rank companies that fit. Or you can start from a single company you already have in mind and run a full deal memo on it. Either way you get the same multi-perspective treatment rather than one analyst's opinion.

It pulls live financial data (fundamentals, filings, prices, insider and institutional activity, and strategy-specific screeners) from Financial Modeling Prep, so the agents reason over real numbers rather than stale training data.

## How It's Built

It runs on Next.js 15 and React 19 with Supabase for data and auth. Mastra orchestrates the agents and the workflow state. The council deliberately mixes models, using Gemini, GPT-4o, and Claude across different roles so that no single model's tendencies dominate the final call.

## What I Took From It

The structure around the models is what made it work. Forcing independent critique, and mixing models so they disagree, surfaces risks that a single bullish report never would. Routing everything through an explicit verdict step is what turns a pile of analysis into a decision you can actually use.
